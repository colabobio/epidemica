# ADR-0000: Title in the imperative

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-XXXX | Rejected
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:**
- **Supersedes / Superseded by:** —

## Context

What problem forces a decision now? What constraints apply — team size, existing code, collaborator
commitments, regulation? State facts, not preferences. Link to the specific files or systems that
motivate this.

## Decision

The decision, stated in one or two sentences, in the active voice: "We will …".

Then any detail needed to make it actionable.

## Consequences

**Positive** — what this makes easy.

**Negative** — what this makes hard or expensive. Be honest; an ADR with no costs listed is a sales
pitch, not a decision record.

**Neutral** — what merely changes.

## Open questions

Anything unresolved that does not block acceptance. If something *does* block acceptance, the status
stays `Proposed`.

## Validation

How we will know this was right or wrong, and by when. Not every ADR needs this, but decisions
resting on an assumption should say what would falsify it.
